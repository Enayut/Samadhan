> Companion to `ATTRIBUTION.md`. Result of a full scan of the bundled
> `../mine_documents/CCL_Annual_Report_2024-25.pdf` (412 pp) for photographic content, done with
> `pdfimages -list` (color JPEGs; `image`/`icc`/`rgb` colour spaces, grayscale masks excluded) plus
> the PDF text layer for page context. Run date: 2026-09-06.

## Headline finding

**This edition contains no dedicated full-page "opencast operations / HEMM" glossy plate.**
The report's photographs are concentrated in the front matter and events, and as **small montage
photos (~300–340 px wide)** inside the Management Discussion chapters (safety, training, CSR,
environment, vigilance). Full-page (~817×1057 px) plates exist only on the covers and a few
front/rear divider pages. For guaranteed high-resolution HEMM/bench imagery, use
`IMAGE_SOURCES.md` §B/§C (CCL/CIL galleries, Wikimedia Commons) — the bundled report is best for
**branded CCL context shots and safety/field/event scenes**.
