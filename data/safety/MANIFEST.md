## Synthetic operational detail (must be badged SIMULATED/SYNTHETIC in-app)

The mine's **Slope Monitoring Plan** specifics (weekly cadence, EX-07 extensometer/MPBX at Bench 3B,
alarm threshold 2.5 mm/day, weekly report to the Regional Inspector) are **plausible operational
procedure consistent with Circular 02/2020** — but the specific instrument identity, thresholds and
readings are **synthetic demo data**. The app's intake extraction panel and task instructions must
carry a "derived from DGMS(Tech) Circular 02/2020 · mine procedure simulated" note where these
values appear.
